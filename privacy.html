HEATR SPORTS

Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Heatr Sports is operated by Paul Lane. Contact us at support@heatrapp.com with privacy questions or requests.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we receive an account identifier and the phone number or identity details you use with Apple or Google sign-in. We collect the answers you choose to give during onboarding, including leagues, bet types, research habits, goals, betting results, and whether you have used an analytics app. Betting results are optional. We do not ask for your name, age, or income in onboarding.

We receive subscription status, product, trial, purchase, renewal, and transaction information from Apple and our subscription provider. We do not receive your full payment-card number. We also process app-use and diagnostic information, support messages, and any chat questions or bet slips you submit.

How we use it

We use this information to sign you in, protect your account, personalize sports research, present eligible subscription offers, verify access, answer support requests, prevent abuse, and diagnose problems. Your answer about prior analytics-app use places your account in a paywall price test; that assignment remains with the account. We do not use reported betting losses to choose a price.

Service providers

Supabase hosts account and app data; Twilio sends phone verification codes; Apple and Google provide sign-in or purchases; Superwall presents paywalls and processes subscription events. When you use an AI chat feature, the question and relevant context may be sent to OpenAI to generate an answer. We share information with these providers only to operate the relevant feature. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and subscription information while your account is active. We keep support and diagnostic information only as long as needed to handle requests, troubleshoot, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. After a verified account-deletion request, we delete or de-identify account data within 30 days, except records we must retain for billing, fraud prevention, or legal obligations. Those retained records are kept only for the required purpose and period.

Email support@heatrapp.com to request access, correction, export, or deletion of your information. Deleting a Heatr account does not cancel an Apple subscription; you must manage or cancel it in your Apple account.

Security and age

We use access controls and encrypted connections to protect information. Heatr Sports is intended for adults who may legally use sports betting information where they live. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new effective date here and give additional notice for material changes when appropriate.
